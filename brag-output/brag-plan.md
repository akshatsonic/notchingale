# Brag Plan: Notchingale

## What is this app?
Notchingale is a native macOS workspace utility pinned right to the hardware notch, bringing tasks, a focus timer, a scratchpad, and calendar events into an instant hover-activated popover.

## The angle
Menu bar icons get lost in invisible overflow on modern MacBooks. Notchingale takes over the physical notch, unlocks the unused screen real estate, and delivers an instant 4-card workspace with zero click friction.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A clean Mac desktop top-edge with a MacBook notch cutout. A small pill anchors right beneath the notch. Headline: "Stop losing apps in your menu bar."

## Key moments (the middle)
- The zero-click hover unfold: GPU Core Animation mask expands smoothly into the 4 pastel cards.
- The 4-card workspace: Sage Green Tasks, Lavender Focus with dot-matrix timer, Ochre Notepad, Slate Blue Events.
- The Notch Timer: Starting a focus session turns the hardware notch pill into a live dot-matrix countdown right below your webcam.

## Outro / punchline
The geometric bird logo shines with: "Notchingale. Native Swift & SwiftUI. 100% Local."

## User flow worth showing
1. Hover notch pill -> 4-card workspace unfolds downward.
2. Start focus timer -> dot matrix activates.
3. Move away -> pill docks at notch showing live countdown.

## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: polished
- Creative direction: Sleek native Apple utility film
- Angle: Modern Mac users lose status items in menu bar overflow. Notchingale solves this by hugging the physical notch, expanding on hover into an instant 4-card workspace, and showing a live focus countdown right under the webcam.
- Hook: "Stop losing apps in your menu bar."
- Outro: Geometric bird logo, "Notchingale", "Native Swift & SwiftUI • 100% Local".

## Storyboard
1. Scene 1 (0.0s – 3.5s): Hook — MacBook screen edge with hardware notch and dark pill. "Menu bar overflow? Put your workspace at the notch."
2. Scene 2 (3.5s – 8.0s): Reveal — Pointer hovers over pill. Downward GPU mask unfold reveals the 4 pastel workspace cards with real UI elements.
3. Scene 3 (8.0s – 13.5s): Action — Focus timer activates with dot-matrix numerals. Panel docks back, leaving a live timer countdown glowing under the notch.
4. Scene 4 (13.5s – 18.0s): Outro — Origami bird logo with metallic sheen, "Notchingale" title, and product badges.
